📋 Mandatory Documents for International Shipping (KYC)

As per Indian customs and aviation regulations, the following documents are required at the time of booking:

  • Sender's Identity Proof: Self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card, Passport, or PAN card.
  • Receiver's Complete Details: Full overseas name, street address, postal code, and active local phone number.
  • Detailed Packing List & Invoice: Itemized list of parcel contents and estimated value for customs clearance.
  • For Medicines: Original Doctor's Prescription with patient name matching receiver, plus original chemist cash memo/tax bill with batch numbers.

⚠️ Strictly Prohibited Air Aviation Items

Aviation security strictly bans the following items in international courier flights: liquids, perfumes, deodorants, lithium batteries / loose power banks, flammable materials, gold/silver bullion, currency notes, and non-certified chemicals. Contact our desk for clarity on any specific item.
